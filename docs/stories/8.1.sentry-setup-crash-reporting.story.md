## Epic Context
**Epic 8: Sentry Crash Logging, PostHog Analytics & Loops Email Sequences - Post-Launch Enhancement**
This story is part of Epic 8, a post-launch epic focused on integrating Sentry for crash logging, PostHog for product analytics, and Loops for automated email sequences. This story implements Sentry integration for automatic crash reporting and error tracking.

**Note:** There are existing stories 8.1 and 8.2 in the repository from a previous Epic 8 (Account Management). This story is for the new Epic 8 (Sentry/PostHog/Loops integration) and should be implemented after Epic 7 is complete (app launched).

## Story
**As a** product manager  
**I want** Sentry integrated for crash logging  
**So that** crashes are automatically captured with stack traces and I can debug issues without manual log checking
